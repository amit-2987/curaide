Data Security & Compliance

Built for healthcare's highest standards.

Curaide is engineered from the ground up with enterprise-grade security and full HIPAA compliance, so your data and your patients' data are always protected.

HIPAA Compliant by Design

All Curaide systems, workflows, and data pipelines are architected to meet HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules. Business Associate Agreements (BAA) available for all covered entity partners.

End-to-End Encryption

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256. PHI is never stored in plain text or exposed to third-party AI model training pipelines.

SOC 2 Type II Certified

Independently audited for security, availability, and confidentiality controls. Our SOC 2 Type II certification validates that your operational data is handled to the highest enterprise standard.

Role-Based Access Controls

Granular, role-based permissions ensure each team member only sees the data relevant to their function. Multi-factor authentication and single sign-on (SSO) supported across all environments.

Audit-Ready Evidence Ledger

Every decision made by Curaide is recorded in a tamper-evident evidence ledger. Full auditability for compliance reviews, payer audits, and internal governance requirements.

PHI De-identification & Segmentation

Protected Health Information is de-identified for analytics workflows. Strict data segmentation ensures no PHI crosses into general AI training data, preserving patient privacy at every layer.
